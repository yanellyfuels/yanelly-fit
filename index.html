Who coaching is for

A thoughtful fit matters.

Applications help me understand your goals and whether your needs fit the current coaching scope. If your needs fall outside that scope, I may refer you to a Registered Dietitian, physician, therapist, or other qualified professional.

A good fit

  • Women age 18 or older
  • Women who strength train regularly
  • Women seeking general nutrition education, structure, and accountability
  • Women willing to communicate honestly and consistently
  • Women ready to learn how to eat for their goals

Not the right fit

  • Anyone seeking diagnosis or treatment of a medical condition
  • Anyone needing eating-disorder treatment
  • Anyone requiring medical nutrition therapy
  • Anyone expecting an extreme diet, detox, or guaranteed result

05 FAQ

Questions before you apply

Who is coaching for?

Coaching is designed primarily for adult women who strength train and want general nutrition education, structure, and accountability to support their goals.

Are you a Registered Dietitian?

No. I am currently a Human Nutrition and Foods student at the University of Houston working toward becoming a Registered Dietitian. My coaching focuses on general nutrition education, habits, and accountability and does not include medical nutrition therapy.

Do you provide meal plans?

My goal is to help clients understand how to make choices and build meals that fit their preferences and goals. Depending on the final service structure, I may provide examples, templates, or meal-building guidance rather than a rigid one-size-fits-all plan.

Do I need to track macros?

Not necessarily. The approach may differ depending on the client, their goals, and what is appropriate within the coaching service.

Can you treat medical conditions or eating disorders?

No. Coaching is not medical care and is not a substitute for treatment from a physician, Registered Dietitian, therapist, or other qualified healthcare professional.

Does applying guarantee acceptance?

No. I review each application to determine whether the person's goals and needs are appropriate for my current coaching service.
